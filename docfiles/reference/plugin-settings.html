Plugin Settings

Project Settings → Plugins → Server Queue, stored in DefaultGame.ini under [/Script/ServerQueueCore.ServerQueueSettings]. Many settings can be overridden on the server command line.

Slots and queue​

SettingDefaultCommand lineDescription
Total Slots16-QueueTotalSlots=The server's capacity
Reserved Slots0-QueueReservedSlots=Part of Total Slots kept for higher priorities
Lend Reserved SlotsOffRegular players may use free reserved slots
Reserved Slot Min PrioritySupporterLowest priority that uses reserved slots
Max Queue Length100-QueueMaxLength=Invited party members count
Max Party Size4-QueueMaxPartySize=1 turns parties off
Party Form Timeout60 sHow long a party waits for its members
Reservation Timeout30 s-QueueReservationTimeout=How long a reserved slot waits for its player
Reconnect Grace Period120 s-QueueGracePeriod=How long a player who left keeps their slot; 0 turns it off
Queue Reconnect Timeout60 sHow long a waiting player keeps their place after losing the connection
Allow Direct JoinOnPlayers with no reservation may log in while a slot is free for them
Position Update Interval1 sHow often waiting players get their position

SettingDefaultCommand lineDescription
